## Founding Story
Diana didn't set out to start a consulting practice — she kept getting the same call. Founders spinning their wheels trying to "do something with AI." After living every failure mode as a 2x CTO, she built Sunbeam to bring clarity, momentum, and trust to AI product work. AI isn't magic, but with the right focus and leadership, it can be transformative.
