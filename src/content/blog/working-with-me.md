If your team is exploring AI but is unsure where to start, you're not alone.

I talk to a lot of startup leaders (especially in B2B SaaS and healthtech) who feel stuck when it comes to GenAI or ML:

> "We know AI matters, but we're not sure what's worth building."

> "The team is experimenting, but nothing's landed yet."

> "Our board keeps asking about our AI strategy, and we don't have a great answer."

Totally normal. I've seen this from the inside as a 2x startup CTO, ex-Amazon engineer, and MIT PhD. Even smart, motivated teams can get lost in the noise.

**That's why I work with founders and tech leads to bring clarity and momentum to AI efforts.**

I'm not big on hype and buzzwords. Just level-headed thoughtful guidance to figure out:

- What's worth building for your customers
- How to move fast (without the rabbit holes and waste)
- How to ship features that people actually trust and use
- The mindset shifts required to succeed with GenAI
- And how to make your AI/ML strategy compelling to your board

If this sounds familiar, I'd love to connect. Happy to trade notes or hear what you're working on.
