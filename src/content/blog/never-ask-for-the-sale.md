I'm still buzzing a little from hosting [Sue Heilbronner](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sueheilbronner/) for a talk about her new book, [Never Ask for the Sale](https://www.amazon.com/Never-Ask-Sale-Supercharge-Ambivalence-ebook/dp/B0DSBK8BP5/). My product book club plus some founders crowded into my living room for the evening.

![Sue Heilbronner speaking about Never Ask for the Sale](/images/blog/2026-never-ask.jpeg)

On the surface, this is a book about sales and how to close a deal. The central concept is using **"passionate ambivalence"** to sell: showing up energized and excited about a project or client relationship, while at the same time remaining calm and unattached to whether a particular deal or engagement happens.

This approach deeply resonates with me. I have a lot of "ick" when it comes to the hard sell, and this approach is more subtle.

But the book goes much deeper than how to sell. Sue focuses on mindset throughout, and discusses **limiting beliefs** and **upper limit thoughts**, which are barriers to how we show up in the world and how we do our best work.

During the book discussion, Sue had us each present a 30-second bio. She challenged us to identify and tell our most compelling version of what we do best, and to really distill our essence. This required a real willingness to go deep and get uncomfortable.
